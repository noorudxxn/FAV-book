new notice

The school management is pleased to announce that the Annual Science and Cultural Exhibition will be held next Friday in the school auditorium from 10:00 AM to 3:00 PM. Students from all classes will participate by presenting innovative science models, art exhibitions, and cultural performances. Parents and guardians are warmly invited to visit the exhibition and encourage the students. All participants are requested to report to their respective classrooms by 8:30 AM in complete school uniform. Students should maintain discipline throughout the event and cooperate with teachers and volunteers. Refreshments will be available during the lunch break. Certificates and prizes will be awarded to the best performers at the end of the program. We encourage everyone to make this event successful through active participation and teamwork. Please contact your class teacher for any additional information regarding the event schedule or participation guidelines.
